Everything for the weekly shop, nothing else

By aisle and by store

The list sorts itself in the order you walk the supermarket. Add your own stores, each with its tab.

Swipe to the cart

Tap or swipe right to tick, left to delete, long-press to edit. Every action can be undone.

Add in seconds

"milk x2, bread, eggs x12" in one go, suggestions as you type, your usual items one tap away, and new items with their aisle.

Works with no signal

Changes show instantly, stay on the phone and are sent when the connection comes back.

Shared at home

Everyone sees the same list within seconds, on their phones and on a tablet dashboard in the kitchen.

History and receipts

"Shopping done" saves the trip with the amount and a photo of the receipt. Repeat any past trip.

About to run out

It learns how often you buy each thing and suggests it when it's due again.

Stats and templates

Spend per month and store, how often you shop, what you buy most, and templates for the lists you repeat.

Your list, on your server

This isn't a service you sign up for: each household runs its own copy. No account, no ads, no analytics.

  • The list, the history and the receipt photos live in your PostgreSQL, so they're in your backups.
  • Access with a token or Cloudflare Access, checked by the app itself: with neither, it refuses to start.
  • Strict security headers and a read-only container with no Linux capabilities.
  • Open source (MIT): you can read every line.

Install yours

One Docker container (web app + API) next to PostgreSQL. At home, on a NAS, a Raspberry Pi or a VPS.

  1. Download and buildgit clone https://github.com/davidniin/grocery-list.git
    cd grocery-list && docker build -t grocery-list .
  2. Connect it to PostgreSQLSet DATABASE_URL and a long GROCERY_TOKEN (or Cloudflare Access). The database schema is created on start.
  3. Add HTTPS and open it on your phoneWith Cloudflare Tunnel, Caddy or Tailscale. Then "Add to Home Screen".
